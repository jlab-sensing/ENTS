# Zulip Community

The ENTS project uses [Zulip](https://zulip.com/) for team communication, technical discussions, deployment coordination, and community support.

---

## Joining the Discussion

All contributors, researchers, field operators, and interested community members are welcome to join our Zulip organization:

* **Zulip Organization URL**: [https://ents.zulipchat.com/](https://ents.zulipchat.com/)

---

## Primary Channels and Streams

Our Zulip organization is organized into topic-based streams:

* **#general**: Broad project announcements, high-level discussions, and general inquiries.
* **#development**: Architecture debates, pull request coordination, firmware debugging, and backend feature planning.
* **#hardware**: PCB layout reviews, component sourcing, enclosure prototyping, and physical assembly QA.
* **#deployments**: Real-world field reports, sensor calibration observations, environmental monitoring updates, and troubleshooting.
